## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ground rules, product principles, and competitive
landscape for StayPilot. It is the governing reference for all future requirements
and implementation.

## 2. Ground Rules

- **Documentation-first:** No feature is implemented before requirements are approved.
- **Business-first:** Solve operational problems before adding AI.
- **Single source of truth:** Documentation overrides implementation assumptions.
- Multi-tenant SaaS from day one.
- API-first architecture.
- Security and privacy by design.
- Every feature requires user stories, business rules, acceptance criteria, database
  impact, and API contract.
- Prefer modular architecture with clear bounded contexts.
- Every breaking change requires versioning and a migration plan.
- Measure success with business KPIs, not only technical completion.

## 3. Product Principles

- AI augments humans.
- Mobile-first for operational staff.
- Fast onboarding (< 30 minutes).
- Simple UX over feature overload.
- Extensible through integrations.
- Audit every important action.

## 4. Competitive Research

| Competitor | Primary Market | Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunity for StayPilot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudbeds | Hotels / PMS | Complete PMS, channel manager, payments | Can be complex for small operators | Simpler AI-first experience for boutique properties |
| Mews | Hotels | Modern cloud PMS, automation | Enterprise focus | Affordable boutique-first offering |
| Guesty | Vacation rentals | Excellent multi-property management | Focused on rentals rather than resort operations | Deeper housekeeping and maintenance |
| Hostaway | Vacation rentals | Channel management, automation | Less operational depth | Operational OS with AI insights |
| Little Hotelier | Small hotels | Easy PMS | Limited AI capabilities | AI concierge and predictive insights |

## 5. Strategic Positioning

StayPilot will position itself as the **intelligent operating system for boutique
hospitality (5–100 units)**. Rather than competing solely as a Property Management
System, it will combine PMS, operations, guest engagement, and practical AI into a
unified platform.

## 6. Initial Differentiators

- AI Operations Manager with daily recommendations
- Native housekeeping and maintenance workflows
- Guest WhatsApp AI concierge
- Predictive maintenance
- Revenue and occupancy insights
